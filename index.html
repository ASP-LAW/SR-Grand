Director's Message

Our Director is a first-generation entrepreneur whose journey is defined by vision, perseverance, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Beginning with his own savings and without external financial support, he built every venture through hard work, determination, and confidence.

His entrepreneurial journey began with a brick kiln business before expanding into fuel retail, construction, infrastructure and finally hospitality through Grand SR Hotel. Every milestone reflects his commitment to quality, integrity and long-term relationships.

Today, Grand SR Hotel stands as a symbol of refined comfort and genuine Indian hospitality. His vision continues to expand into real estate, education and infrastructure while creating employment opportunities and contributing positively to society.

Mr. S. R. Singh

Founder & Director
